Educator · Accessibility agent · Maker of autonomous access

Gregory Zobel

I work across education, accessibility, open practice, caption studies, and experimental publishing — treating access as both an ethical commitment and a creative method.

One practice, two volumes. The scholarship, the caption advocacy, the open courses, the AAA zines, and the web experiments are not separate careers — they are different instruments playing the same question: who gets access to knowledge, media, tools, and participation?

→ Two ways in

  • Academic, Teaching & Research

    Captioning and accessibility scholarship, open educational resources, and teaching. Restrained, citable, credible.

  • Art, Access & Experimental Publishing

    The Association of Autonomous Access — zines, détournements, caption fiction, posters, and the web experiments. Louder, stranger, on purpose.
