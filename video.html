Video experiments

Video

Kinetic détournements, accessibility definition shorts, and AAA propaganda slides. Short, silent or captioned, made to be shared. All self-hosted — no third-party trackers.

Every clip has a text version — open “Text version” under any clip to read its on-screen text. All clips are silent, so no captions are needed. Self-hosted, about 13 MB total.

01 Kinetic détournements (1–27)

Silent, looping text animations — hijacked phrasing re-aimed at captioning, labor, and access. Clips 1–15 are the constructivist art series; 16–27 read in order as a twelve-part manifesto.

02 Accessibility definition shorts

Short clips defining core captioning and accessibility terms — for learners outside a traditional classroom.

03 AAA propaganda shorts & slides

Short, slide-based agit-prop on captioning labor and AI — from the Transcripts of the World series.
