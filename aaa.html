Association of Autonomous Access

Art & AAA

Anti-copyright print and agit-prop that re-aims the politics of access — captioning, non-speech information, and captioner labor — at anyone with a photocopier.

[MANIFESTO] Access is not a feature to be granted. It is a commons to be seized, copied, and handed on. Take what you need. Reproduce freely. Credit no landlord.

02 Détournements

Famous quotes hijacked whole and re-aimed at closed captioning, captioner labor, and AI displacement — the canonical strikethrough → revised → gloss form.

  • [REDUB] Marx, Warhol, Sartre, Crowley, and Keanu, redirected toward access politics.
  • [REVISED] Compiled into small books for free distribution.

Many more détournements are published on my Substack, and a full collection — The Invisible Hand Writes… — is available on Gumroad. Animated versions live on the Video page.
