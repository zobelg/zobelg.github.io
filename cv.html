Research & Scholarly Interests

Captions and captioning; accessibility and Universal Design; usability of online education; user experience and usability testing; video and screencasting in online learning; educational technology and emerging technologies (including AI); eLearning and open-source tools; Open Educational Resources (OERs); research-methods training in technical communication and usability; and graduate-student professionalization.
