Academic, Teaching & Research

Scholarship

Captioning and accessibility, Universal Design, usability of online education, and open educational resources.

01 Research & scholarly interests

Captions and captioning; accessibility and Universal Design; usability of online education; user experience and usability testing; video and screencasting in online learning; educational technology and emerging technologies (including AI); eLearning and open-source tools; Open Educational Resources (OERs); research-methods training in technical communication and usability; and graduate-student professionalization.

05 About & CV

Gregory B. Zobel, Ph.D., is Associate Professor of Educational Technology at Western Oregon University (Division of Education and Leadership, College of Education). He holds a Ph.D. in Technical Communication and Rhetoric from Texas Tech University (2011), an M.A. in English from Humboldt State University (2006), and a B.A. in English from the Robert D. Clark Honors College, University of Oregon (1995). He currently coordinates the MSEd: Educational Technology & Instructional Design program.
