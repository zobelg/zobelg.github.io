Academic, Teaching & Research

Scholarship

Captioning and accessibility, Universal Design, usability of online education, and open educational resources.

01 Research & scholarly interests

Closed captioning [CC] and captioning; transcripts; accessibility and Universal Design; usability of online education; user experience and usability testing; video and screencasting in online learning; educational technology and emerging technologies (including AI); Open Educational Resources (OERs); and graduate-student professionalization.

09 About & CV

Gregory B. Zobel, Ph.D., is Associate Professor of Educational Technology at Western Oregon University (Division of Education and Leadership, College of Education). He holds a Ph.D. in Technical Communication and Rhetoric from Texas Tech University (2011), an M.A. in English from Humboldt State University (2006), and a B.A. in English from the Robert D. Clark Honors College, University of Oregon (1995). He has been MSEd Program Coordinator since June 2024 and has coordinated the Educational Technology & Instructional Design certificate since 2020. He led the 2023–2024 revision that became the MSEd in Learning Design & Technology and co-led the 2026 merger of three MSEd programs into the MSEd in Curriculum & Instruction Design. Earlier roles include Director of the Program for Undergraduate Research Experiences (2018–2020), Faculty Senate President (2018), and Chair of the Graduate Studies Committee (2017–2018). Current service includes the Graduate Studies Committee, the DEL Program Review Committee, and the Digital Accessibility Policy Subcommittee (2024–2025). He has supervised more than seventy-five master’s capstones since 2021.
